GIROO

Founded in 2024, Giroo creates trendy and eco-friendly eyewear for young consumers using biodegradable materials and recyclable packaging. Competing with brands like Privé Revaux, Quay Australia, and Warby Parker, Giroo stands out through its affordable pricing and distinctive mint-inspired branding. While the eyewear market is highly competitive, Giroo has opportunities to grow by strengthening its social media presence and increasing brand awareness among its target audience.
